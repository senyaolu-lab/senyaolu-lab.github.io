02 / Ready-to-use campaign descriptions

Short synopsis

The City Has a Hold on You is an independent Deadlock fan campaign exploring the game's atmosphere, memorable heroes and shared player moments through a coordinated collection of artwork, editorial guides and community prompts.

Extended synopsis

The City Has a Hold on You approaches Deadlock as a place to discover and a cast to remember. The independent fan project brings together a master visual, three hero spotlights, a gameplay reading guide, social artwork, wallpapers and a screenshot challenge under one editorial identity.

Infernus, Dynamo and Ivy give the collection three different points of entry: fire and momentum, control and timing, and the value of support. The campaign pairs expressive fan-created language with sourced information, making room for both curiosity and clarity.

The collection is a creative exploration of Valve's game. It does not announce a release, distribute access or represent Valve. Readers seeking current game availability should consult Deadlock's official Steam page.

One-line credit

05 / Useful answers

Is this an official campaign?

No. This is an independent fan-created design and editorial project. The tagline, community format and creative framing originate with this project.

Can this project provide access to the game?

No. Use the official store page to check Valve's current availability information. The project does not collect Steam credentials, sell access or operate an invitation service.

Are these all gameplay screenshots?

No. Read each asset's label. Original promotional compositions and concept art are not evidence of a playable scene. Official game imagery should retain its source attribution.

Where should someone ask about this creative project?

Use the comment or reply channel on the original post through which the campaign was shared. No press email address or representative has been invented for this package.

06 / Sharing and source notes

  • Describe the project as an independent fan campaign wherever context might otherwise imply an official release.
  • Keep Valve's authorship of the underlying game distinct from the fan project's original layout and copy.
  • Use the source register to locate original material. Public availability is not a blanket grant of reuse rights.
  • This kit does not grant rights to Valve assets, third-party fonts or other contributors' work. Check the applicable source terms for the intended use.
  • For community submissions, obtain the contributor's separate permission before reposting or adding work to a downloadable package.
  • Do not add review scores, invented endorsements or an access offer to these materials.
