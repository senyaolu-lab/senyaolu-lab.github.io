Streetlight Stories / Operating plan

One frame.
Your perspective.

A focused screenshot challenge for existing players who want to show a detail of Deadlock that deserves another look.

THE CITY HAS A HOLD ON YOU. / NON-VIDEO CAMPAIGN
Planned fan activity · Not currently running

01 / The prompt

Show us the city through your eyes: one original Deadlock screenshot, one title, and a caption of up to 40 words explaining the detail you noticed.

The activity rewards attentive looking through thoughtful discussion and an optional editorial selection. It has no monetary prize, product prize, invitation reward or access offer. There is no sign-up form. People participate by replying to a single host post.

02 / Operating format

NameStreetlight Stories
Entry windowFive consecutive 24-hour periods from the original host post's visible publication timestamp. The timestamp, not a repost, defines the window.
Entry locationReplies to the original host post. One public thread is the source of record.
EntryOne image, a title of up to 10 words, a caption of up to 40 words, and a note that the entrant captured it.
Who can participatePeople able to provide their own permitted game capture and participate under the host platform's rules. This activity supplies no game access.
Editorial resultUp to three optional featured entries, subject to separate permission. There may be fewer, including none, if suitable permissions are not available.

03 / Entry rules

  1. Use your own capture. Do not submit someone else's screenshot, an official publicity image or a generated image as your own.
  2. Keep the scene intact. Cropping, straightening, modest exposure adjustments and privacy redaction are allowed. Composite scenes, inserted objects, generative fills and paintovers are outside this screenshot challenge.
  3. Protect people. Remove private chat, personal information and any information that could identify someone beyond their chosen public handle.
  4. One entry per person. You may replace it before the window closes. Clearly label the new reply “Replacement entry”; only the latest eligible entry will be considered.
  5. Use the current public playtest responsibly. Follow the game's applicable terms and the platform's rules. Do not use leaks, private development material or content obtained through circumvention.
  6. Keep the conversation welcoming. No targeted harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, spam or access-selling.
  7. Do not manufacture engagement. Likes, follower counts and paid promotion do not affect editorial scoring.
  8. Reposting is a separate choice. Submission lets the host view and discuss the entry in the thread. The host must ask before copying it into a new post, gallery or downloadable file.

04 / Selection rubric

Two reviewers independently score eligible entries where possible. An entry can be striking without being technically elaborate. Evaluate the image and caption together, with no preference for expensive hardware or editing tools.

CriterionWeightWhat earns a strong score
Atmosphere and attention40 pointsA distinct detail, a sense of place, or an observation that makes the viewer look again.
Composition and readability35 pointsA clear focal point, deliberate framing and an image that reads well at ordinary screen size.
Caption connection25 pointsA concise, specific caption that adds context or feeling rather than repeating the obvious.

Method: score each criterion from 0–5, then multiply by 8, 7 and 5 respectively. Average the two reviewers' totals. Resolve a tie using the higher atmosphere score, then the higher caption score. If still tied, feature both where practical or document a short joint editorial choice.

If only one reviewer is available, disclose that in the recap. Do not imply independent judging. This is a curated fan exercise, not a scientific ranking or a popularity contest.

05 / Host sequence

  1. Prepare: choose the host account, confirm a moderator is available, and test that image replies are supported. Attach the rules and matching prompt card.
  2. Open: publish Post 08. Record the timestamp and calculate the five-day end point. Keep any cross-posts pointed to this thread.
  3. Respond: acknowledge entries, answer practical questions and use Post 09 as a composition tip. Avoid promising selection.
  4. Close: add a reply stating that the entry window has ended. Freeze the eligible-entry list; allow later replies as conversation only.
  5. Review: check eligibility, score entries and seek permission from the proposed selections.
  6. Share: publish only approved selections, with the requested public credit and a link to the original entry. State the number of entries reviewed only after counting them.
  7. Reflect: record participation, recurring questions and what should change for another run.

06 / Moderation decisions

SituationAction
Incorrect format or missing capture statementAsk once for the missing detail and allow correction within the entry window.
Unclear authorshipAsk for clarification. Exclude from selection if authorship remains unresolved; do not demand account credentials.
Private information visibleHide or remove the entry where the host has permission; ask the entrant to repost a redacted version.
Targeted abuse, threats or spamApply platform moderation tools and document the action. Do not quote or amplify the harmful content.
Good-faith criticismLeave it visible and respond to the substance. Criticism of the campaign is not grounds for exclusion.
Permission declined or unansweredDo not repost. Selection can remain unfilled.
Withdrawal requestRemove the entry from consideration and remove host-controlled reproductions already posted. Explain any limits on third-party copies.

07 / Ready-to-use host messages

Permission request

We would like to include your Streetlight Stories entry in a fan-campaign recap post, with your chosen public credit and a link to your original reply. May we repost this image and caption for that purpose? Saying no will not affect your participation. We will not add it to a downloadable kit without asking separately.

Entry-window closure

The Streetlight Stories entry window has closed. Thank you for sharing the details that made you stop and look. We are reviewing eligible entries and will ask contributors before reposting any selections. New replies are welcome as conversation.

Correction request

Thanks for taking part. Please add a short note confirming that you captured this screenshot yourself. You can update your entry before the original submission window closes.

08 / Record only what is useful

Maintain a simple host log: public entry link, public credit, eligibility decision, criterion scores, permission status, and any correction or withdrawal request. Avoid collecting real names, email addresses or Steam credentials. Keep only the public information needed to run and document the activity.

Community approach informed by GameMaker's community and marketing guidance. The prompt, rules, scoring and host messages above are original proposals for this independent project.
