Project identity

This is an independent creative project about Valve's Deadlock. The campaign line, layouts, editorial framing and proposed activity are fan-created. They do not represent a new game mode, a release announcement or an official Valve initiative.

Game status belongs in the factsheet, where it is linked to the official source. Check that source again before publication.
