Creative proposal · Publication-ready editorial system

01 / The idea

A place can catch your eye. A character can become your favorite. A moment with a team can bring you back. This campaign gives each of those attractions a clear, memorable place.

THE CITY HAS A HOLD ON YOU. is original fan campaign language. It treats the city as the connective tissue between atmosphere, heroes and player stories. It is not presented as canonical dialogue or Valve's official tagline.

PILLAR 01

The city calls

Make architecture, strange details and the game's visual identity worth noticing. Let the first image invite a closer look.

Lead assets: master key visual, city study, wallpapers.

PILLAR 02

Find your character

Give three heroes distinct editorial personalities. Lead with a readable silhouette and one strong thought.

Lead assets: Infernus, Dynamo and Ivy spotlights.

PILLAR 03

Share the moment

Help newcomers notice movement, timing and teamwork. Invite existing players to contribute a moment of their own.

Lead assets: gameplay primer, screenshot challenge, community selection.

04 / The visual voice

07 / What success means

Judge the work by understanding and participation. A viewer should identify the game, remember one distinct visual or character, and know where to find reliable information. An existing player should have a clear, low-friction way to join the conversation.

  • Creative recognition: show a piece briefly and ask what the viewer remembers.
  • Useful attention: compare saves, shares and meaningful replies per impression.
  • Action: track clicks from the campaign's own page to the official information source.
  • Participation: count eligible screenshot submissions, distinct contributors and permissioned selections.
  • Accuracy: keep the source register current and log any factual correction.

Measurement boundary: Steamworks UTM Analytics requires product-specific reporting permissions. This project does not claim access to Valve's conversion data.

08 / Final review

  • Every public-facing sentence is in English.
  • Hero names, visual identities and source labels match.
  • Headlines remain legible on a phone and in a small thumbnail.
  • The unofficial identity is visible without overwhelming the artwork.
  • Official facts link to an appropriate source; fan interpretation is identified.
  • Game screenshots and original concept imagery are clearly distinguished.
  • Every piece has a job in the rollout and a suitable destination.
  • No release date, review quote, access guarantee or prize has been invented.
  • Links, text wrapping, image crops and print pages have been checked.

Campaign process references: Steamworks marketing practices and GameMaker's marketing guide. Their principles inform this proposal; its creative choices are independent.
