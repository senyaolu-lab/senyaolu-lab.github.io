Planning document · No posts scheduled or published

Day 01 is the day the host chooses to begin, after final asset review. This plan creates no automation and assigns no calendar date. Keep one primary social channel and one accessible collection page; use additional channels only when someone can respond there.

01 / Before Day 01

  • Open every delivered file and check it on desktop and phone.
  • Verify current availability against the official Steam page.
  • Confirm source credits and distinguish original art from actual gameplay imagery.
  • Upload the final collection and files to the chosen destination, then test every link.
  • Select the host account and moderator for Streetlight Stories.
  • Decide which platform metrics and own-page events can actually be measured.

Challenge timing

Publish the challenge on Day 08. Its five-day window closes exactly 120 hours after that post's timestamp, on Day 13 at the corresponding time. Complete review and permission checks before sharing any selected work. If permission is still pending on Day 14, publish Post 12 without submissions and share an approved gallery later.

03 / A small, honest measurement sheet

MeasureDefinitionWhat it tells you
Useful engagement rate(Shares + saves + substantive replies) ÷ impressions × 100, where those metrics exist.Which pieces generate attention beyond passive viewing. Compare within the same channel.
Click-through rateRecorded link clicks ÷ impressions × 100 for a given post.Whether the message creates enough interest to leave the feed.

Record unavailable metrics as not available, not zero. Separate paid and organic distribution if any advertising is later authorized. Use the first releases as a baseline; do not invent numerical targets or guaranteed outcomes.

04 / Review rhythm

After each release

Check broken links, legibility and factual questions first. Respond to useful comments. Note the time of measurement so comparisons use a similar age since publication.

At Day 07

Review the first six posts. Identify which message and asset family drew the clearest responses. Adjust the next captions without changing the campaign identity.

At Day 14

Record strongest pieces, common questions, actual participation and work that took more effort than expected. Recommend one focused follow-up theme.

After a correction

Update the affected file, page and pinned information. Add a brief correction where the original claim was published; keep the source register aligned.

05 / Measurement boundary

Own-page activity and public-platform engagement can support this fan campaign's evaluation. Official game wishlists, purchases and activations require appropriate Steamworks reporting access. Steam's UTM documentation also excludes Playtest app conversion reporting. Do not infer game acquisition from a click.

Sources: Steamworks UTM Analytics; Steamworks marketing practices. The timetable, asset sequence and review measures are this project's recommendations.
